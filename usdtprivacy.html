  • Information shared when contacting support.

  • Simulated mining activity such as hash rate values and calculated rewards;

  • Survey and offer completions submitted through third-party survey/offerwall partners, and the in-app coin rewards credited as a result;
  • Music listening task progress and completions, and the in-app coin rewards credited as a result (see Section 2.7 below);

2.4 Device and Technical Information

  • Device model and operating system version;
  • App version and configuration details;
  • IP address and approximate geographic location;
  • Advertising identifiers and installation identifiers;
  • Crash logs and diagnostic reports.

2.5 Information from Third-Party Services

The App integrates trusted third-party services including Google Play Services, Google Firebase (authentication and database), Firebase diagnostic tools, advertising partners such as Google AdMob, AppLovin, Start.io, Yandex, and survey/offerwall partners such as TheoremReach and CPAlead (see Section 2.6 below). These providers may collect and process data according to their respective privacy policies.

2.6 Survey and Offerwall Partners

The App includes an optional "Surveys" section that lets you complete surveys and offers provided by third-party survey/offerwall partners, currently including TheoremReach and CPAlead, in exchange for in-app coin rewards. When you choose to participate:

  • You leave the App's own interface and interact directly with the partner's website or survey content, displayed within the App;
  • The partner may collect information such as your device/advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, demographic answers you provide (e.g. age range, gender, interests), and survey/offer completion status;
  • We share a unique, non-identifying account reference (your app user ID) with the partner solely so that completed surveys/offers can be matched back to your account and the correct coin reward credited;
  • The partner's own privacy policy, not this Policy, governs how they collect, use, and store the information gathered during a survey or offer. We encourage you to review each partner's privacy policy before participating:
    • TheoremReach: https://info.theoremreach.com/privacy-policy/
    • CPAlead: https://www.cpalead.com/privacy-policy
  • Participation in the Surveys section is entirely optional and is not required to use the rest of the App.

2.7 Music Listening Tasks (Watch & Earn)

The App includes an optional "Music Task" section that lets you listen to a song in the Spotify or YouTube Music app to earn in-app coin rewards. When you use this feature:

  • The App checks, locally on your device only, whether Spotify and/or YouTube Music are installed. This check stays on your device, is never transmitted to us or any third party, and is used solely to guide you to install the relevant app if it isn't already present;
  • While a music task is active, the App monitors, at the device system level, whether any audio is currently playing (a simple yes/no signal). The App does not access, read, log, or transmit what song, artist, or content is actually playing, and does not access your Spotify or YouTube Music account, login, or activity in any way;
  • Your listening progress (the number of seconds detected as actively playing toward a specific task) is stored and linked to your account, so that your reward can be accurately calculated and credited once the required listening time is completed;
  • We do not send any information to Spotify or YouTube Music, and we do not receive any information from them beyond the on-device "is audio playing" signal described above;
  • A persistent notification may be shown while a music task is in progress, displaying your live listening progress for that task;
  • Participation in the Music Task section is entirely optional and is not required to use the rest of the App.
