About the Studio 02

Independent.
Intentional.
Built to last.

BlockWave Studios is a solo Android app studio based in India. No investor pressure, no feature bloat — just focused engineering on applications people actually use.

Our mission is straightforward: build apps that respect the people using them. That means honest privacy practices, clean performance, and interfaces that stay out of the way. Every release is held to the same standard.

We're a small operation, and we think that's a strength. Decisions get made faster, quality stays tighter, and nothing ships until it's ready.

Studio Principles

  • 01
    Performance by default

    Every app is tuned to be smooth and responsive on real-world devices, not just benchmarks.

  • 02
    Privacy without compromise

    Minimal data collection, clear policies, and nothing your users wouldn't approve of.

  • 03
    Security built in, not bolted on

    Sound engineering choices from the start — not an afterthought when something goes wrong.

  • 04
    Long-term reliability

    We stay with our releases. Updates keep coming, bugs get fixed, and the app improves over time.
