About the Studio

Just me,
building things
properly.

BlockWave Studios is an independent Android app studio based in India. The studio was built around a clear mission: design and release Android applications that are fast, secure, and genuinely respectful of the people using them.

Every application is approached with the same standard — reliable performance across real devices, transparent privacy practices, sound security engineering, and a user experience that remains clean and uncluttered.

Applications are maintained and improved after launch. Quality takes precedence over quantity, and nothing ships until it meets the studio's standards.

  • Performance is non-negotiable

    Every release is tested across real-world devices. Applications ship when they are fast and stable, not before.

  • Privacy by default

    Data collection is kept to the minimum required. Policies are written plainly and followed strictly.

  • Security from the ground up

    Sound engineering decisions are made early in the process, not applied as a fix after something goes wrong.

  • Reliability after release

    Applications are supported and updated after they launch. Continuous improvement is part of the process.
