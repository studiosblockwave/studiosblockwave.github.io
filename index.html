BTC Miner

🚀 BTC Miner – Experience Virtual Crypto Mining. BTC Miner lets you explore how cryptocurrency mining works in a simple and interactive way. Start your virtual mining, increase your mining speed with boosts, and watch your mining progress grow over time. This app is designed for people who are curious about Bitcoin mining and want to understand the concept through a safe environment.

Available on Google Play Regular Updates Privacy Focused Smooth Experience Beginner Friendly
Get it on Google Play

BTC Miner recreates the mining process using a virtual system. All mining activity happens inside the safe environment and does not use your device hardware for real mining. You can start mining with a single tap, activate boosts to increase mining speed, and track your virtual progress directly from your dashboard.
